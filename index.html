WHAT IS NEUROSAATHI

NeuroSaathi is India's first complete neurodevelopment ecosystem — a single place bringing together everyone involved in a child's development journey: the parents raising them, the doctors and therapists treating them, and the special educators teaching them. Instead of families spending ₹5,000–₹15,000 and weeks of searching to piece together screening, professional referrals, government scheme guidance, and daily-life support on their own, NeuroSaathi brings it into one place — most of it free to start.
