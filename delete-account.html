NeuroSarathi Account & Data Deletion

If you'd like to permanently delete your NeuroSarathi account and associated data, you can request this at any time by contacting us directly.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to info@neurosarathi.com from the email address linked to your NeuroSarathi account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Email Us to Delete My Account
Please include your registered email address and, if applicable, your child's profile name so we can locate and remove the correct records.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account credentials and login information
  • Your child's profile information (name, age, developmental data, routines, milestones)
  • Screening and check-in responses
  • Chat history with Sarathi Avatar

What May Be Retained

  • Payment transaction records, as required for tax and financial compliance (typically retained for the legally required period, then deleted)

Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within a reasonable timeframe, and confirm once it's complete via email.

Questions?

Contact us anytime at info@neurosarathi.com.
