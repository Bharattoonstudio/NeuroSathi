# NeuroSarathi — VISION.md

This document answers four questions. Every proposed feature should be checked against this before it is checked against anything technical.

---

## 1. Why does NeuroSarathi exist?

Because a parent who has just been told their child may be autistic, or has ADHD, or a speech delay, is usually handed a diagnosis and very little else. They are left to search the internet at 2 a.m., piece together government schemes, find a therapist, figure out a daily routine, and do all of it alone — often in a language, city, or income bracket the existing system wasn't built for.

NeuroSarathi exists to be the guide that walks beside that parent from the first worried search onward — not a diagnosis tool, not a replacement for a doctor, but the thing that makes the next step clear.

## 2. What problem are we solving that nobody else solves?

Most products in this space are one of:
- A directory (find a therapist, nothing else)
- A clinical tool (built for professionals, not parents)
- A generic parenting app (not built for neurodivergent kids specifically)
- A forum (opinions, not verified guidance)

NeuroSarathi's bet is that no one has built a single place that is simultaneously: India-first (schemes, language, culture), honest about uncertainty (not pretending an app can predict a child's future), and genuinely daily-use (routines, reminders, real progress tracking) rather than a one-time screening tool a parent visits once and forgets.

## 3. What will we never do, even if competitors do?

- We will never fabricate a statistic, confidence score, or prediction to make the product look smarter than it is.
- We will never let the product imply it can diagnose. It supports, educates, guides, tracks, and explains — it does not replace a doctor, therapist, or psychologist.
- We will never use guilt, urgency, or shame to drive engagement. No fake streak pressure, no "your child is falling behind" language.
- We will never bury what data we store or how it's used in legal text no parent will read. Privacy is a visible feature, not a footnote.

## 4. How should every parent feel after using NeuroSarathi?

More informed than when they arrived. Less alone. Clear on one small next step, not overwhelmed by fifty. And — even on a hard day — not judged.

If a feature doesn't move a parent toward one of those feelings, it doesn't belong in the product, no matter how technically impressive it is.
